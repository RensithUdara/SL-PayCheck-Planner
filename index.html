My employer uses a different EPF %

Statutory minimum is 8% (employee) + 12% (employer). Some employers elect to contribute at higher rates under Section 11 of the EPF Act, or run an Approved Private Provident Fund (APF) with its own rates (e.g. 10%/15%) — enter your actual rates here if they differ.
